# Next Player

Next Player plays a media queue locally or on a Cast receiver. Playback can continue while the user browses the app.

## Language

**Playback session**:
The active media queue and its playback state, including the current item, position and playback output.

**Mini player**:
The compact view of an active playback session available while browsing the app.

**Cast controls**:
The playback controls for a session whose output is a Cast receiver.

**Expanded player**:
The expanded view of a playback session, showing its artwork and playback controls. Local video is watched through the separate full-screen video player.

**Connecting to Cast**:
The period after selecting a receiver and before it acknowledges the selected video with available playback controls. Phone playback is paused during this period.

**Full-screen video player**:
The view for watching video on the device running Next Player.

Synthetic compatibility fixtures; no bytes from the user's recording are included.

header-program-stream.mp4: 4500-byte synthetic IMKH/zero prefix plus MPEG-PS/H.264.
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=160x120:rate=10 -t 3 -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 10 -f mpeg synthetic.mpg
  Prefix synthetic.mpg with b'IMKH' + bytes(4496).
compatibility-opus.mkv and compatibility-vorbis.mkv:
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=160x120:rate=10 -f lavfi -i sine=frequency=440:sample_rate=48000 -t 4 -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 10 -c:a libopus compatibility-opus.mkv
  Replace the audio codec with vorbis -strict experimental -ac 2 for compatibility-vorbis.mkv.
compatibility.flac:
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i sine=frequency=440:sample_rate=48000 -t 2 -c:a flac compatibility.flac

The FLAC fixture also includes a generated 16x16 blue PNG cover, marked attached_pic,
to verify artwork is not mistaken for an unsupported video track.

Generate the PNG separately with -frames:v 1, then remux it into the complete FLAC
using -map 0:a -map 1:v -c copy -disposition:v attached_pic (without a frames limit).
The completed FLAC fixture has 94 audio packets and a two-second duration.

compatibility-hdr.mp4: three-second synthetic AVC/PQ video for rejected-reader recovery.
8-bit AVC exercises the HDR transfer guard without requiring a Main 10 decoder on CI.
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=160x120:rate=10 -t 3 -an -c:v libx264 -pix_fmt yuv420p -g 10 -bsf:v h264_metadata=colour_primaries=9:transfer_characteristics=16:matrix_coefficients=9 -color_primaries bt2020 -color_trc smpte2084 -colorspace bt2020nc compatibility-hdr.mp4

Legacy fixtures use only FFmpeg testsrc2 and sine generators (160x120, 25 fps,
four seconds, stereo 48 kHz; no original recording bytes):
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=160x120:rate=25 -f lavfi -i sine=frequency=440:sample_rate=48000 -t 4 -ac 2 -g 12 <options> <filename>
  legacy-mpeg1.mpg: -c:v mpeg1video -c:a mp2 -f mpeg
  legacy-xvid.avi: -c:v mpeg4 -vtag XVID -c:a libmp3lame
  legacy-wmv2.wmv: -c:v wmv2 -c:a wmav2
  legacy-dvd.vob.mpeg: -c:v mpeg2video -c:a pcm_dvd -f vob
LegacyVideoCompatibilityTest covers content-URI metadata, all uncached thumbnail
strategies, and SW audio/video rendering with forward/backward seeks. Optional
compatibilityVideo tests a local original through both content and file URIs.
CompatibilityPickerTest also accepts compatibilityDuration (ms),
compatibilityWidth, and compatibilityHeight for original-specific assertions.
The HW+ Xvid test checks rendered Android MediaCodec video with SW audio, then
checks PlayerService keeps HW+ video while recovering an Android audio failure.

legacy-layer1.mpg: MPEG-2 video plus valid silent MPEG Audio Layer I frames.
  Generate 500 frames, each bytes.fromhex('ffff4000') + bytes(132), as silence.mp1.
  ffmpeg -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=160x120:rate=25 -i silence.mp1 -t 4 -c:v mpeg2video -g 12 -c:a copy -f mpeg legacy-layer1.mpg
Auto startup tests verify that DVD LPCM and MPEG Layer I keep their actual audio
formats through the compatibility reader; these clips contain no user media.
